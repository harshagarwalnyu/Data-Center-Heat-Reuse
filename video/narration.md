Every year, about seven hundred seventy-eight gigawatt-hours of heat leave a data center on Cayuga Lake and vanish into the air.
Now the Lansing town board has directed its attorney to draft a ban. Free heat alone is not enough to earn a yes.
So we built a live model. Sell the heat to neighbors, and cooling always wins. A farm campus comes first, at about forty-one dollars a megawatt-hour. Then five hundred homes. The town center fails the cost test, so it is not built.
Every number rolls live from the model. Drag the cost of money. At seven percent, our heat costs a hundred and six dollars, against a hundred and thirty-six for propane. At four percent, with community co-op finance, about ninety.
