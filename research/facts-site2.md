## 8. Surprises (Contradictions & Vulnerabilities in PLAN.md)

1. **Water Argument Pushback Confirmed & Amplified:**  
   *PLAN.md §1* warned that "the water argument is weaker than it reads." Research proves it is even weaker:
   - TeraWulf and Lake Hawkeye LLC's stated design is a **closed-loop dry-cooling system** with recirculating fluid that does not withdraw or discharge lake water for daily operations.
   - The DEC water permit renewed in April 2026 for 1.008 MGD is specifically designated for coal plant site maintenance, sump pumping, and dust control remediation—**not** continuous data center cooling.
   - Any claim that exporting waste heat saves "1,000,000 gallons of lake water per day" is factually incorrect and would be immediately dismantled by judges. Waste heat reuse saves dry-cooler fan electricity and winter boiler fossil fuels, not lake water. The water argument should focus strictly on protecting Cayuga Lake from potential thermal runoff and preserving watershed integrity, rather than claiming a 1:1 displacement of water withdrawal.

2. **Existential Town Ban & Legal War (October 2026):**  
   The project is in acute regulatory jeopardy. On September 29 / early October 2026, the Lansing Town Board directed its attorney to draft an outright **prohibition/ban on data centers**, setting aside $500,000 in litigation reserves. An Article 78 lawsuit brought by FLX Strong and CLEAN survived a motion to dismiss in Tompkins County Supreme Court in April 2026.
