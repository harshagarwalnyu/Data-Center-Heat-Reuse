## How this maps to the product

The app in `PLAN.md` is a Next.js story: the fight → supply ≫ demand → site map → Sankey → temperature ladder → seasonality → household savings → ownership → shutdown scenario → carbon and water → the ask. Explore mode is live sliders (electricity price, air vs liquid cooling / COP, uptake %, discount rate, DC load). Delivery is presenter mode plus an offline fallback, a public URL, a short video, and a one-page leave-behind with a QR code.

This note turns published UX, accessibility, and data-story practice into rules for that app.
