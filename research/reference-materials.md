Design this file maps against (from `PLAN.md`, draft 2026-10-03 — internal plan, not an external source):

- Working site: proposed ~400 MW campus at the former Cayuga coal plant, Lansing, NY.
- Cooling reliability: data-center cooling never depends on offtakers. Heat recovery is a sidestream. Primary heat rejection is dry coolers, not the lake.
- Two thermal products: a ~70°C anchor loop (central heat pump) and an ambient loop near source temperature (building-level water-to-water heat pumps).
- Residential ambient loop is gated on linear heat density, not the whole town.
- Storage: hot-water tanks sized for 4–12 h of anchor peak. Seasonal borehole store is a scenario only.
- Demand-side design: start from who needs heat. Heat price must beat what Lansing households pay today per delivered kWh after heat-pump electricity.
- Quantified value for the data center, heat users, and community; cost, risk, and ownership sharing; exit if the data center shuts down.
