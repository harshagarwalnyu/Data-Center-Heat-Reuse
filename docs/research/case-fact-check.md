## DG-EX — Deep Green, Exmouth Leisure Centre

**Verdict: the March 2023 writeup faithfully reports the three pages it cites. Those pages give expected first-year figures, not a metered operating year. On 2026-10-04 the company’s own site no longer lists Exmouth as live capacity.**

The source file’s conflict notes hold:

- Savings wording is “more than £20,000” (Channel Vision) and “£20,000 ($24,000)” (DCD). The BBC page has neither figure. Leave them as a conflict.
- Carbon unit word is “25.8 tons” (Channel Vision) and “25.8 tonnes” (DCD). The numeral matches. The unit word does not. Do not convert one into the other.
- Electricity: BBC says Deep Green refunds the centre. DCD says Deep Green pays upfront at the customer’s hourly rate. Read together, the pool is described as not paying for server power and not paying for heat. Neither page is a contract.
- BBC’s “seven other England pools had signed up” is a signed-pipeline claim. DCD’s move from a target of seven to a target of 20 is a target claim, plus “signed” projects in Bristol and Manchester. Those are different sentences. They are March 2023 statements, not later completion records.

**ASSUMPTION (arithmetic only):** 139,284 / 222,000 = 62.7 percent, which the articles round to 62 percent. 139,284 kWh / 8,760 h = 15.9 kW average if the expected heat were spread over every hour of a non-leap year. The articles do not state operating hours or a load factor. The source file’s “about 16 kW” line is that arithmetic, and it is below the 28 kW nameplate. 28 kW is 0.028 MW.

No public capex for the Exmouth install appears on the three pages. The 96 percent recycle figure appears on the Channel Vision page and not on the BBC or DCD pages.
