# Nielsen 10-heuristic review — Thermal Commons pitch app

**Interface:** Thermal Commons story / explore / compare / print (Site 2 Lake Hawkeye vs Site 1 111 8th Ave)
**Audience:** 50–70-year-old executives, projected in a room (large type, one takeaway, plain English)
**Date:** 2026-10-04
**Method:** Expert heuristic evaluation of `web/screenshots/*.png` (Nielsen, 1994). Severity 0–4.
**Scope note:** Findings below are filled as each screenshot is inspected. Until a screenshot is named in a finding, it has not been reviewed.

## Audience and task

Judges (HDR + Grundfos) and senior reviewers watch a 15+ minute Story pitch, then may open Explore sliders, Compare, and a Print sheet. Success = they can state the ask, the site, the heat path, and one number they trust — from the back of the room.
