Audience: a 65-year-old Grundfos vice president, opening the public URL alone, with no presenter. Goal: decide whether Thermal Commons is a serious heat-reuse proposal they can defend to HDR and to Grundfos colleagues.

Method: Nielsen-style cognitive walkthrough against page code and captured screens. Each step records what they see, what they can honestly understand, and where the path breaks. Fixes are ranked by whether they change that decision.

Judging map: HDR regenerative lenses (Community, Ecology, Health; human health, community, air, carbon, water, biodiversity, nutrients) and Grundfos as a pumps / hydronic-systems company.
