Policy Options
Incentives
Offer tax credits for the reuse of data center heat.
Provide grants or low-interest loans for data center heat reuse.
Prioritize permitting and/or interconnecting data centers that reuse their heat. 
Enabling Policies
Support projects that demonstrate the technical feasibility of data center heat reuse. 
Establish matching platforms for data centers and heat end users.
Leverage local and regional planning to co-locate data centers and heat offtakers.
Develop district thermal energy networks to provide heating and cooling to neighborhoods and campuses 
as an alternative to on-site thermal generation. 
Standards
Establish energy efficiency standards for data centers that allow data center heat reuse to meet the 
requirements.
Require plans for heat reuse in permitting of new data centers.
Establish a fee for a data center’s greenhouse gas emissions from electricity consumption.

2026 Opportunities & Wins
•
Virginia
•
Passed legislation directing the Department of Energy to conduct a study on the opportunity for data 
center heat reuse in the state.
•
Illinois
•
Illinois POWER Act would set requirements for data centers; opportunity to include heat reuse in energy 
and water provisions.
•
California
•
Legislation introduced that would allow utilities to operate thermal energy networks; received some 
opposition.

Projects in the U.S. and Europe
•
Syracuse University project 
•
Reusing heat from its on-campus data center for space 
heating in an adjacent office building. 
•
Notre Dame University project
•
Reusing heat from its on-campus data center for a 
proximate greenhouse.
•
Westbank project in San Jose, CA
•
Reusing data center heat in a district heating system for a 
4,000-unit residential complex. 
•
Microsoft Greenhouse Warming Project in 
Amsterdam
•
Reusing heat from a Microsoft data center at nearby 
greenhouses.
