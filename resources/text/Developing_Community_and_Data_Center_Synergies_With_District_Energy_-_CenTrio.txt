Data Center Developments Facing Increased 
Community Resistance
• Nationally Data Center developments are facing increased 
community scrutiny due to concerns over
• Rising utility costs
• Water consumption
• Responsible community stewardship
• 25+ projects cancelled nationally due to community backlash
• Rising resistance to AI as a technology


--- page 4 ---
How can Data Centers Benefit their 
Communities
• The discussion on the value of data center developments has 
centered around potential financial benefits
• Incremental Property Tax revenue
• Monetization of non-productive land
• Job creation
• Communities are no longer accepting these as rationale for new 
developments

--- page 6 ---
Challenges
• Technical design scope is 
constantly shifting
• Cooling and heating requirements are 
dependent on the off takers and the 
technology available which is evolving 
rapidly
• Data center off-takers need to be 
comfortable with non-traditional 
cooling solutions
• New financial structures and risk 
structures being developed
• Community education and 
communication
• Balancing new low-carbon drivers 
with mature approaches


--- page 7 ---
Opportunities
• The district plant provides cooling to the data center and captures the waste 
heat from the data center for the rest of the development
• The data center generates the revenue that makes the entire development 
viable/investable
• Several attempts at developing an adjacent site have failed
• The community gains an asset and energy source and a driver for further 
development
• The data center gains community support and access to some of the most 
important data center real estate in the country 
• A potentially replicable model can be developed for similar mixed-use 
developments across the country
